Pace

Support

Pace is an offline workout tracker for iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers and no tracking: your workouts stay on your device.

Contact

Common questions

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings › Your data lets you create an encrypted backup and restore it on another device. Keep the backup password safe: it cannot be recovered.

Can I import from Strong?

Yes. Settings › Your data › Import accepts a Strong CSV export and shows a preview before anything is saved.

I forgot my backup password.

Backups are encrypted on your device, so neither Pace nor the developer can recover the password or the backup.

Face ID or App lock isn't working.

Check Settings › Pace › Face ID in iOS Settings is enabled. If Face ID fails, Pace offers your device passcode.

Sounds or rest-timer alerts are missing.

Check Pace's Sound setting, your iPhone volume and notification permission in iOS Settings. In-app sounds play even when the silent switch is on.

Does Pace need an account or subscription?

No. Pace is free, has no purchases and works fully offline.
